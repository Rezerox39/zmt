About

ZMT is a free, open-source music player for Android with a terminal-inspired (TUI) interface. It supports local audio files, Jellyfin streaming, Telegram channel sync, and YouTube Music playback. Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and ExoPlayer (Media3). Dark AMOLED-optimized UI with true black backgrounds.

Features

🎧 Local Playback

MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WAV, AAC, OPUS — bundled FFmpeg decoders for every format.

📡 Jellyfin Streaming

Stream your personal music server. Covers, lyrics, format info, and playback stats.

🤖 Telegram Sync

Connect any public Telegram channel as a music source. Browse and stream directly.

▶️ YouTube Music

Search and stream from YouTube Music via yt-dlp + Innertube. Download tracks to device.

🎨 AMOLED Themes

Pure black backgrounds, monospace typography. Terminal classic and red accent themes.

📝 Synced Lyrics

TTML and LRC lyrics with karaoke highlighting, translation, and LRCLIB auto-fetch.

🚗 Android Auto

Full Android Auto support: browse tracks, albums, folders, voice search.

📊 Listening Stats

Play counts, time played, most played tracks. Cue sheets and m3u8 playlists.
